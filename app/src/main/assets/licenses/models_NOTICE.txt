On-device models used for masks

sam_encoder.onnx, sam_decoder.onnx
  MobileSAM (https://github.com/ChaoningZhang/MobileSAM), Apache License 2.0
  (see mobilesam_Apache-2.0.txt), built on Segment Anything by Meta AI (Apache License 2.0).
  ONNX export: https://huggingface.co/Acly/MobileSAM (MIT). The decoder is stored with
  8-bit weights (ONNX Runtime dynamic quantization).

sky.onnx (built by CI with tools/models/sky_model.py)
  DeepLab v3+ Xception65 trained on ADE20K, from the TensorFlow Models DeepLab model zoo
  (https://github.com/tensorflow/models/tree/master/research/deeplab), Apache License 2.0.
  Converted to ONNX; the output is reduced to the probability of the "sky" class.

lama.onnx (fetched by the build)
  LaMa: Resolution-robust Large Mask Inpainting with Fourier Convolutions
  (https://github.com/advimman/lama), Apache License 2.0. ONNX export:
  https://huggingface.co/Carve/LaMa-ONNX (Apache License 2.0).
